# UC Evidence Companion — evidence-base gap assessment

Source: `ibd-prototype-evidence.json` (prototype-v1) — 20 sources, 49 included claims, 12 excluded, 7 flagged with limitations.

## Critical gap: no UC-only claims exist

Every claim's `conditionApplicability` is one of:
- `ibd_general` (29 claims)
- `crohns_disease` only (15 claims)
- `ulcerative_colitis; crohns_disease; ibd_general` combined (5 claims)

For a UC-scoped product, only the 34 `ibd_general`/combined claims are usable as UC evidence. The 15 Crohn's-only claims must never be shown as UC evidence.

## Specific coverage holes

No UC-eligible evidence exists today for any of the following, even though they are core patient questions:

- **Fecal calprotectin and CRP** — the only claims that exist (CLM-066, CLM-061) are tagged Crohn's-only.
- **ESR** — no claim at all in the package.
- **Mucosal healing, colonoscopy, intestinal ultrasound** — no claims.
- **Any treatment/drug class** — biologics, JAK inhibitors, loss of response, maintenance-of-remission drug therapy: zero claims anywhere in the package.
- **`symptoms_vs_inflammation` topic** — all 5 claims are Crohn's-only, so UC users get no direct evidence on "can symptoms occur without active inflammation."

## What IS well covered for UC (at ibd_general/combined level)

- General UC/IBD background (`core_condition_knowledge`)
- Diet-and-flare questions: fibre, ultra-processed food, probiotics, dietary patterns, fruit/vegetables, food additives, alcohol, dairy/lactose
- Stress and quality-of-life evidence

## Recommended next research priorities

Prioritize sourcing and reviewing new evidence specifically tagged `ulcerative_colitis` (not just `ibd_general` or `crohns_disease`) for:

1. Fecal calprotectin
2. CRP
3. ESR
4. Mucosal healing
5. Colonoscopy / intestinal ultrasound monitoring
6. Biologics
7. JAK inhibitors

These are the gaps currently blocking full coverage of the "tests and monitoring" and "treatments" question categories in the UC Evidence Companion product spec.
